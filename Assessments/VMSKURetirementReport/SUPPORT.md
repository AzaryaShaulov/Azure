# Support

This is a community project, provided as-is under the MIT license.

- **Questions and bugs:** open a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/AzaryaShaulov/Azure/issues). Please sanitize any
  tenant, subscription or VM identifiers first.
- **Security issues:** see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Azure retirement dates and policies:** the authoritative source is Microsoft, via the
  [retirements list](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/virtual-machines/sizes/lifecycle/retirements-and-capacity-restrictions) and
  [End of Life sizes](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/virtual-machines/sizes/lifecycle/end-of-life-sizes-list).
  For account-specific questions, contact Azure support or your Microsoft account team.

This project is not an official Microsoft product and is not supported through Microsoft support channels.
